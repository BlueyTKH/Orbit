# Changelog

# Orbit 3.0.0 — Public

NotchFlow becomes Orbit: a broader home for notch controls, battery information and desktop widgets.

- New Orbit name/icon and refreshed Settings with reorganised navigation, page colours, typography, search and appearance themes.
- Native configurable battery widgets: rings/list/bars, four sizes, device selection/order, Automatic lowest-first, monochrome and progress options.
- Connected accessory collection plus optional selected-device Bluetooth battery-service discovery and trusted iPhone/iPad USB/Wi-Fi reads.
- Existing preferences/keychain identity retained; legacy saved-data folders migrate to Orbit with conflict recovery and merged battery histories.
- Matching personally named phone/tablet discoveries and overlapping battery readings consolidate across Settings, menu and widgets; existing widget selections follow the merged device.
- AirPods listening-mode registry fallback runs away from the interface thread and drains its report before waiting, avoiding a pipe deadlock.
- Charging bolt placed beside the percentage inside the menu-bar battery.

Existing notch animations and dimensions are preserved. Device/proximity support and WidgetKit refresh are OS/device dependent. Detailed iPhone health, Apple Watch readings, universal native-alert suppression and general-download notarization are not claimed by this release preparation.
